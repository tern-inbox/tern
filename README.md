# Tern

Email for iPhone and Mac that sorts your inbox into mail that needs a reply and mail that's just for your information. It works with Gmail and iCloud, and newsletters get their own place.

- **iPhone:** [Tern Inbox on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tern-inbox/id6832356359) (free)
- **Mac:** [download the latest DMG](https://github.com/tern-inbox/tern/releases/latest). It's signed and notarised, and needs macOS 26.
- **Website and support:** https://tern-inbox.github.io

This repo is only where the Mac downloads live.
